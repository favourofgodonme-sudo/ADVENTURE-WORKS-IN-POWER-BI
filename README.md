# ADVENTURE-WORKS-IN-POWER-BI
A data analyed to get the total budget in different years, sum of tax amount, sum of sales and more in a super market by name Adventure works to help solve the business problem 
I started with understanding the power query editor which includes the ribbon, queries pane, data preview and formula Bar to help me easily carry out my data
And then I went into Power query transformation library, in this stage I was able to remove duplicates, change datatypes, trim and clean, replace values, remove columns and more.
I also loaded my data before starting the analysis 
I went into creating relationships between the budget, sales, products, territories, calendar and customers 
after which I began to create my visuals to make the data to be easily understand, at the end of the analysis I was able to get the sum of budget, sales amount and tax amount for each year, which helped me to discover the business problem and give a recommendation.
